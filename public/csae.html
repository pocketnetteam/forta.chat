Child Safety Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Updated at July 28th, 2026

This page sets out the publicly published standards of Forta Chat (operated by Dvm Analytics LLC) against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to the Forta Chat application and related services available on Google Play and elsewhere.

Zero tolerance

Forta Chat has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation. We strictly prohibit any content, conduct, or activity that involves, depicts, solicits, promotes, or facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors, including CSAM in any form (images, video, text, audio, links, or other media).

Users must not create, upload, share, store, request, or otherwise distribute CSAE or CSAM through Forta Chat. Violation of these standards will result in enforcement action, which may include removal of content, suspension or termination of accounts, and referral to law enforcement or relevant authorities where required or appropriate.

Age and eligibility

Forta Chat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly allow users under 13 to use the Service. If we become aware that a user under 13 is using Forta Chat, we will take steps to remove or disable that account as appropriate. Parents or guardians who believe a child under 13 is using the app should contact us at the address below.

Addressing CSAM

When Forta Chat obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or other CSAE on our platform, we will take appropriate action in accordance with these standards and applicable law. Such action may include, without limitation:

  • Removing or disabling access to the reported content;
  • Restricting, suspending, or terminating the accounts of users involved;
  • Preserving relevant information as required by law; and
  • Reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or other relevant regional authorities, as required or appropriate under applicable child safety laws.

Compliance with child safety laws

Forta Chat and Dvm Analytics LLC are committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations related to CSAE and CSAM, including processes to report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant authority in the applicable jurisdiction.

How to report CSAE / CSAM

Anyone who encounters content or behavior on Forta Chat that may involve child sexual abuse or exploitation should report it immediately.

  • Email: support@forta.chat (subject line: “CSAE Report” or “Child Safety”)
  • Please include as much detail as reasonably possible (for example: room or conversation identifiers, usernames, timestamps, and a description of the content), without redistributing illegal material.

Users may also contact us through the in-app feedback / support channels available in Forta Chat where provided.

Child safety point of contact

For notifications from Google Play, law enforcement, or other parties regarding CSAE content related to Forta Chat, the designated child safety point of contact is:

Dvm Analytics LLC — Child Safety Contact
Email: support@forta.chat

This contact is authorized to discuss Forta Chat’s enforcement and review procedures and to take action when required.
